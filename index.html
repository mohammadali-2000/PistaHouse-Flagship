CLARITY & TRANSPARENCY

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our recipes, travel packs, shelf life, and international express deliveries.

How do Pista House travel packs stay fresh for 45 days without preservatives? +
We employ advanced NASA-grade retort thermal processing. The prepared Biryani or Haleem is immediately vacuum-sealed into multi-layer foil pouches with high oxygen barriers, then sterilized under controlled pressure steam. This eliminates all bacteria naturally, allowing room-temperature shelf stability for 45 days with zero synthetic preservatives.
Is Pista House Haleem available year-round or only during Ramadan? +
While Haleem is the king of Ramadan, Pista House prepares our authentic GI-Tagged Haleem 365 days a year for our international travel pack export orders and flagship Charminar restaurant due to continuous global customer demand.
How do you ship internationally to USA, UAE, UK, and Canada? +
We operate via temperature-controlled air freight partnerships with Emirates SkyCargo, British Airways World Cargo, and DHL Express. Consignments are packed with thermal insulation and priority customs clearance, arriving at destination doorsteps within 36 to 48 hours.
Are all Pista House meats and ingredients 100% Halal certified? +
Yes, 100%. All mutton, chicken, and poultry utilized across all Pista House kitchens are certified Halal, procured from government-approved slaughterhouses complying with strict Islamic culinary laws and FSSAI standards.
What is the best way to reheat the Heat & Eat Biryani travel pack? +
Option 1 (Microwave): Snip open the pouch, empty the Biryani into a microwave-safe dish, cover loosely, and heat on high for 3 minutes. Option 2 (Boiling Water): Submerge the sealed pouch into boiling water for 5 to 7 minutes. Snip open and serve steaming hot!
Can I place bulk orders for weddings, corporate gifting, or Eid celebrations? +
Yes! We provide customized gift hamper boxes, engraved royal tins, and bulk catering orders worldwide. Simply click our "Instant WhatsApp Order" button or email orders@pistahouse.in to connect directly with our corporate concierge.
